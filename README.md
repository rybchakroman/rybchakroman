# Roman Rybchak

Founder & CEO of [Cardio2](https://cardio2.info), an AI companion for people recovering at home after a heart attack, a stent, heart surgery or a heart failure diagnosis.

I build AI for the months after hospital discharge, when most patients in Europe never receive cardiac rehabilitation. My principle: language models explain and coach, while every safety decision is made by deterministic, clinician-approved rules.
